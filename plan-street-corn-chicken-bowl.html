When you're home: cook it (10 min)

  1. Microwave the rice pouch 90 sec. Use HALF the pouch (save the rest).
  2. Esquites corn, no mayo: drain the can, heat 1 cup in the pan (or microwave) until hot with a little char. Off heat, stir in 2 tbsp shredded cheese, a big lime squeeze, a spoonful of pico, and Tajin or chili powder.
  3. Pull ~6 oz of rotisserie breast, SKIN ON, and slice it.
  4. Bowl it: half-rice, corn, chicken. Top with more pico and a lime wedge.
  5. Macros: ~61g protein / 69g carbs / 23g fat / ~735 kcal - fits today's remaining 790 kcal / 59P / 82C / 24F (fat has ~1g headroom, so no extra cheese).
